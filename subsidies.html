💰 Government Schemes & Subsidies

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

₹6,000 yearly financial support to small & marginal farmers, given in 3 installments.

Soil Health Card Scheme

Helps farmers get their soil tested and improve productivity with proper nutrients.

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance scheme to protect farmers from natural disasters & yield losses.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Provides short-term credit to farmers at low interest rates for farming needs.
